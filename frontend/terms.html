Honor Code & Terms of Service

Last Updated: July 2026 Version 1.2

1. Agreement to Terms

Welcome to UniMart! By registering as a student and accessing or using our campus peer-to-peer marketplace, you agree to comply with and be bound by the following Terms of Service, along with our Campus Honor Code. If you do not agree to these rules, you are prohibited from using this platform.

2. Campus Honor Code

UniMart is built on mutual trust within our student community. Every member is expected to uphold strict academic integrity and personal ethics:

"I pledge to represent my listings honestly, to respect the safety and property of my peers, and to carry out all campus transactions with transparency and respect."

  • Honest Listings: You must describe item conditions accurately (including scratches, wear, or battery issues). Photos must be of the actual product being sold.
  • Fair Pricing: No extreme gouging or deceptive practices. AI price suggestions are provided to help align with standard campus valuations.
  • Meeting Commitments: If you agree to a price and meeting spot via chat, arrive on time or notify the other student in advance. Unsportsmanlike cancelations may result in flags.

3. Transaction Safe Zones

UniMart acts solely as a listing facilitator. We do not process physical exchanges or intermediate payments:

  • Safe Exchange Areas: All physical trades must occur inside verified campus zones (e.g. Canteen lobby, Central Library plaza, or Student Union courtyard). Avoid meetings in uncrowded, private off-campus spaces.
  • Secure Payment: We recommend in-person digital transfers (e.g. UPI, card tap) or cash exchange only after checking and approving the product conditions in person. Advanced online bank deposits are strictly prohibited.

4. Prohibited Items

The following list of listings is strictly banned from UniMart:

  • Alcohol, tobacco, e-cigarettes, weapons, or illegal drugs.
  • Academic cheating materials, homework answers, or copyright-violating course files.
  • Stolen campus equipment, keys, or university security items.
  • Counterfeit bills, credit cards, or unauthorized gift certificates.

Sellers of prohibited items will receive immediate suspensions and may be reported to campus student disciplinary affairs.

5. Student Privacy & Security

We value your privacy. When registering:

  • We only collect essential details (name, university email, hostel block, phone number) to guarantee student authenticity.
  • Your personal phone details are kept hidden and are only exposed if you select to display them or share them in private buyer-seller chat coordinates.
  • We do not sell or monetize student data with third-party advertising companies.
