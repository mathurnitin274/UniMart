Help Center & FAQ

Need assistance? Learn how to navigate UniMart and transact safely on campus.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I search for items available in my hostel block? ▼
You can search for specific hostel blocks by clicking the 📍 Location Selector in the top navigation bar. Simply click on a popular campus area (e.g., Hostel A, Hostel B, Tech Park) or search for your block in the dropdown card. The marketplace will automatically filter listings matching that location.
Is my student registration verified? ▼
Yes! To keep UniMart safe for everyone, only students with verified institution registration are allowed to post items or message sellers. Upon signing up, registration checks are made against university enrollments.
How does the AI Price Suggestion & Description Generator work? ▼
When listing a new item, click the Generate with Gemini AI button after entering the product title, condition, and key specifications. Our integrated AI will generate a professional, optimized product description and recommend a fair pricing range based on current campus market trends.
How do I contact a seller to purchase an item? ▼
Simply navigate to any product details page and click the Chat with Seller button. This opens a secure, real-time message thread where you can discuss the item, negotiate, and coordinate a safe meeting spot to exchange the product.
What do I do if I find a suspicious or prohibited item listing? ▼
If you come across a listing that violates UniMart guidelines (e.g., illegal items, fake listings, harassment, or non-students), please click the Report a Listing link in the footer, select the item, and describe the issue. Our administration panel monitors reports and takes down listings immediately.

🛡️ Campus Safety Guide

Your safety is our top priority. Please adhere to these guidelines for all campus meetups:

  • 📍
    Public Meeting Spots
    Always meet in highly populated, well-lit campus areas. Ideal locations include the library lobby, central canteen, or the main academic quad.
  • 👁️
    Inspect Before Paying
    Examine the item thoroughly in person before making any transaction. Test electronics, flip through books, and verify product conditions.
  • 💸
    No Advanced Payments
    Never transfer money online to a buyer or seller before receiving and verifying the product in person. Beware of prepay requests.
  • 👥
    Bring a Peer
    If you are purchasing a high-value item (like a laptop or cycle), consider bringing a classmate or hostel roommate along to the transaction.
